# IMA Trend-to-Brand Engine

## Purpose
Build an independent, evidence-led capability for discovering emerging consumer needs and translating validated opportunities into responsible product experiments, creator partnerships, commerce and measurable learning. This specification is inspired by publicly described business patterns; it does not authorize copying another company's code, private data, confidential know-how, brands or creative assets.

## Operating loop
**OBSERVE → DETECT → CROSS-CHECK → SEGMENT → SCORE → VALIDATE → DESIGN → TEST → PARTNER (with consent) → PILOT → MEASURE → LEARN → REASSESS**

## Evidence collection
- Use public and authorized sources, official APIs and permitted platform access.
- Record source URL/name, retrieval timestamp (ISO-8601 UTC), language/market, sample limits, methodology and confidence.
- Prefer multiple independent signals; distinguish velocity, durable demand, purchase intent and social controversy.
- Respect privacy, terms, copyright, access controls, rate limits and deletion requests. Do not scrape restricted/private areas or bypass controls.
- Treat every trend as a hypothesis until validated.

## Opportunity scorecard
Score and explain: trend acceleration; demand persistence; audience size and relevance; purchase intent; unmet need; competition/substitutes; price sensitivity; estimated unit economics and assumptions; sourcing/manufacturing feasibility; regulatory and product-safety burden; fulfillment/returns; cultural fit; sustainability; reputation risk; testability; and time to learn.

## Opportunity brief
For each shortlisted idea record: customer problem; audience; independent evidence; alternatives; value proposition; differentiated concept; MVP; cost and margin ranges with assumptions; potential suppliers (only if actually checked); regulatory/safety needs; risks; low-cost test; success/failure thresholds; and next action.

## Creator and community fit
Assess authentic fit between product, creator values and actual audience; public engagement quality; credibility; potential conflicts; brand-safety and reputational risks; usage rights; and disclosure requirements. A creator is not a partner until an agreement is confirmed. No impersonation, fabricated endorsements, fake engagement, spam or undisclosed paid promotion. Outreach, spending, contracts and publication require explicit human authorization.

## Product-to-market pipeline
1. Public-signal discovery.
2. Independent demand validation (search, interviews, waitlist, landing-page test or small permitted pilot).
3. Prototype and quality/safety/compliance review.
4. Unit economics and downside analysis.
5. Limited pilot with clear stop criteria.
6. Production only after validated demand and authorized financing.
7. Distribution, support, returns, inventory and repeat-purchase measurement.
8. Learn from real outcomes; do not infer sales from views or likes.

## Creator-to-commerce measurement
Use authorized tracking links, codes and affiliate agreements; disclose commercial relationships where required; measure funnel conversion, contribution margin, refund/return rates, repeat purchase, fulfillment time, complaints and customer trust. Verify market/platform availability before selecting a channel; never assume TikTok Shop is available in a given country. Consider lawful alternatives such as an owned product page and authorized delivery partners.

## Category safety
Food, supplements, cosmetics, children's products and other regulated categories require category-specific regulatory, labeling, safety and claims review. Never generate unsupported health claims. Do not order, manufacture, pay, publish or enter agreements without explicit authorization.

## Evidence states
- PLANNED: proposed only.
- DISCOVERED: public evidence found, not validated.
- VALIDATED: independent demand evidence meets defined threshold.
- PILOTED: limited experiment actually executed.
- VERIFIED: results supported by primary data and checks.
- COMMERCIAL: actual authorized product/partnership/revenue exists and is evidenced.

## Metrics
Signal precision; time from signal to test; validation cost; conversion; contribution margin; repeat purchase; returns/refunds; fulfillment; creator-to-sale attribution quality; complaint rate; retention; and confidence calibration. Views alone are not product-market fit.

## Privacy and governance
Personal data stays private by default. Global learning may use only permitted, minimized and appropriately aggregated information. Preserve provenance and uncertainty. Provide correction/deletion paths where required. Never claim to have accessed sources or generated revenue without evidence.

## Learning memory entry
Every meaningful run records ISO-8601 UTC timestamp, event, summary, provenance, status (PLANNED/ATTEMPTED/EXECUTED/VERIFIED/FAILED/GAPS_RECORDED), concrete evidence, assumptions, unresolved gaps and next action. Preserve failures and do not silently rewrite history.

## Relationship to IMA
The engine is a module above the canonical IMA core. It must not alter IMA identity, privacy boundaries or truthfulness. Start with research and small validation experiments, then add execution adapters only where supported and authorized. Production capability is not claimed until implemented, tested and verified.
