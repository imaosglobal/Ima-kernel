# IMA Global Integration Bridge

## Purpose
IMA is designed to interoperate with organizations, platforms, research groups, devices and public-benefit networks worldwide. This document defines the bridge without falsely claiming that an external organization has partnered with IMA.

## Integration layers
1. **Protocol layer** — stable IMA contracts, provenance, identity, consent and human-agency gates.
2. **API layer** — adapters for official APIs, SDKs, MCP-compatible tools and webhooks.
3. **Data layer** — normalized evidence, source timestamps, permissions, retention and deletion boundaries.
4. **Agent layer** — model/tool/agent gateways with explicit authorization.
5. **Physical layer** — robotics, wearables, vehicles, sensors and future embodied systems.
6. **Communication layer** — email, messaging, social, video, voice and publishing.
7. **Commerce layer** — catalog, checkout, payouts and accounting evidence; no autonomous money movement.
8. **Research layer** — universities, laboratories, scientific datasets and reproducible experiments.
9. **Public-benefit layer** — NGOs, humanitarian organizations, education, accessibility, civic and cultural institutions.
10. **Continuity layer** — portable identity, memory provenance, versioning and migration across vendors.

## Organization onboarding
For every target:
- identify the official organization and official integration path;
- classify the relationship as API, developer program, research, partner, marketplace, grant, accelerator, open-source, data-sharing, deployment or communication;
- verify terms, geography, eligibility, pricing and privacy;
- prepare the smallest adapter or application;
- request authorization where required;
- authenticate only with authorized credentials;
- run a reproducible test;
- record evidence;
- promote status only after verification.

## Global coverage strategy
Coverage is organized by domain and geography rather than by a finite list of companies. New organizations can be added without changing the core runtime.

Regions:
North America; Latin America; Europe; Middle East; Africa; South Asia; East Asia; Southeast Asia; Central Asia; Oceania; polar research.

Domains:
AI; cloud; robotics; consumer technology; search; communications; media; payments; health; education; science; mobility; space; manufacturing; energy; climate; government; humanitarian work; accessibility; cybersecurity; identity; creative technology; open source; finance.

## Safety boundary
IMA never:
- claims a partnership because an organization is listed;
- creates an account without authorization;
- sends a contract, sponsorship, donation, purchase or paid advertisement autonomously;
- transfers money autonomously;
- exposes private user memory to a third party;
- treats an API key, login page or public profile as proof of a partnership.

The goal is maximum interoperability with minimum lock-in and maximum human control.
