# 1. English — International Announcement

## What if a good life is not about maximizing pleasure or minimizing suffering?

I've developed a new research program asking whether human flourishing may depend on a different relationship between the two.

**Pleasure, Suffering, and Balance — Research Program**

The project examines pleasure and suffering through philosophy, psychology, well-being research, attachment, and the question of what it means to remain consciously engaged with life without allowing either pleasure or suffering to become the sole ruler of action.

This is a research framework and philosophical hypothesis, not a claim that the question has never been studied before.

The first public version was published on September 19, 2026.

**Author:** Ori Cohen  
**Project:** IMA — אמא  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22838744  
**Research release:** https://github.com/imaosglobal/Ima-kernel/releases/tag/pleasure-suffering-balance-v1.0

I am publishing the framework openly so that others can read it, question it, criticize it, develop it, or build research from it.

**Central question:**

Can we learn to live fully without making either pleasure or suffering our master?

# 8. Research Integrity

This outreach package is for discovery and public discussion.

The underlying paper explicitly does not claim that pleasure has never been studied. It recognizes established work on Epicureanism, Aristotelian eudaimonia, utilitarian and hedonist traditions, Buddhist analysis, and modern hedonic/eudaimonic well-being research.

The proposed contribution is a particular synthesis and research program connecting pleasure, suffering, and possible principles of balance.

Readers are invited to examine the sources, challenge the hypotheses, identify prior work, and contribute further research.
