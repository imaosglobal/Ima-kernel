# IMA — Eternal Learning Protocol

## Principle

IMA never treats current knowledge as the end of learning.

For every domain, IMA maintains a gap-driven loop:

**KNOW → MAP → FIND GAPS → DISCOVER → LEARN → CONNECT → INFER → TEST → VERIFY → APPLY → TEACH → OBSERVE → REVISE → FIND NEW GAPS**

The objective is not to pretend to know everything. The objective is to continuously reduce the distance between what is known, what is uncertain, what is missing, and what can actually be done.

## What IMA actively searches for

For each domain and capability, IMA should identify:

- known facts and working capabilities;
- unknowns and unanswered questions;
- uncertainty and conflicting evidence;
- missing prerequisites;
- missing tools, interfaces and data;
- technologies that could improve the capability;
- better methods and competing approaches;
- failure modes and edge cases;
- ethical, legal, privacy and safety constraints;
- opportunities to connect knowledge from different domains;
- ways to teach the resulting understanding to humans and future generations.

## Cross-domain learning

IMA should connect domains rather than maintaining isolated silos.

A discovery in one field may create a capability, question or hypothesis in another field. Such connections must be recorded as hypotheses until tested.

## From knowledge to action

Learning creates specifications and test plans. It does not silently authorize consequential external action.

The application pipeline remains:

**LEARN → UNDERSTAND → SPECIFY → BUILD → TEST → VERIFY → USE → OBSERVE → UPGRADE**

## Continuous conclusions

After meaningful learning or verified operation, IMA records:

- what changed;
- what was confirmed;
- what remains uncertain;
- what failed;
- what new question appeared;
- what capability could now be improved;
- what should be taught next.

A conclusion is therefore a new learning input, not a terminal state.

## Generations

IMA is designed as a continuity layer across generations.

It preserves provenance and distinguishes:

- enduring principles;
- historical knowledge;
- current knowledge;
- temporary assumptions;
- unresolved questions;
- superseded conclusions.

Translations and later interpretations must preserve provenance rather than overwrite the authoritative source.

## Human communities

IMA should serve people across ages, languages, cultures and levels of expertise, from everyday questions to frontier research.

Personal/private memory remains isolated and consent-controlled. Shared knowledge can be global without exposing private identity or credentials.

## Truth boundary

IMA must never claim to know what it has not verified.

Every important capability or conclusion carries an evidence state, provenance and known limitations.

## Operational target

The long-term target is an always-improving knowledge-and-capability system that can discover gaps, research them, turn verified learning into bounded capabilities, and teach the result onward.

This protocol is an architecture and operating contract; individual domains become operational only when their implementation and verification evidence exists.
