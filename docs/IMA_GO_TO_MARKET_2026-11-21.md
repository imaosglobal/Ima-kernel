# IMA Go-to-Market — 2026-11-21

## Objective
Move IMA from capability accumulation into measured market participation while preserving truth, privacy, human agency, provenance and authorization boundaries.

## Initial market wedges
1. Human-centered intelligence pilot — individuals, families, creators and accessibility users.
2. Device integration pilot — device makers, robotics, automotive and smart-home teams.
3. Accessibility intelligence pilot — accessibility, education, care and public-service organizations.
4. Knowledge/technology partnership — technology companies, research groups, creators and startups.

## Market loop
Discover → qualify → close capability gaps → validate → package offer → reach users/partners → pilot → measure → improve → expand.

## Immediate public entry surface
A lightweight /launch.html page is added to the canonical IMA UI as a market entry surface. It does not create a second product or runtime.

## Commercial boundaries
IMA may prepare market analysis, offer definitions, landing copy, pilot plans, partner qualification, outreach drafts and evidence dashboards.
IMA must not automatically sign agreements, spend funds, make binding commitments, disclose private information, claim unverified traction or impersonate a person or organization.

## Success gates
IMA becomes a verified market participant only when evidence exists for real external users or partners, successful pilot delivery, measurable user value, repeat usage or renewal, and a lawful commercial or reciprocal-value mechanism.
