# IMA market entry — canonical launch

IMA now treats market entry as a product loop, not an advertising event:

1. **Discovery:** search, direct links, community, creators and controlled campaigns.
2. **Landing:** the public page states one clear promise and four real jobs.
3. **Activation:** visitor chooses a job, then authenticates before personal chat.
4. **Value:** first useful answer is measured as a candidate event; it is not counted as success merely because chat opened.
5. **Retention:** repeat use and feedback become the next optimization target.
6. **Referral:** users can share a job-specific link back to the same canonical IMA surface.
7. **Revenue:** paid pilots remain experiments until actual payment and repeat value are measured.
8. **Trust:** IMA continues to distinguish documented, implemented, tested, deployed and verified-live capabilities.

The first market promise is intentionally narrower than the full IMA vision: **give IMA a real human need and let it help with the next useful step.**

Current market evidence shows a large and increasingly competitive AI-assistant/companion market, so IMA should compete on a distinct human-centered workflow rather than generic chatbot breadth.
