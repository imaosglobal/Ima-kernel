# IMA Store Protocol

## Purpose

The IMA Store is a distribution layer for verified creations around IMA: digital knowledge, teaching materials, creator kits, extensions, adapters, accessibility resources and eventually physical products.

## Principles

- No product is represented as official merely because it appears in the catalog.
- Provenance and licensing are recorded.
- Safety and privacy are reviewed before publication.
- Paid transactions require a real payment provider and explicit user consent.
- No automatic purchases.
- No deceptive scarcity, fake reviews or artificial endorsements.
- Creator attribution and rights remain explicit.
- Free/open resources can be listed alongside paid resources.
- The catalog can exist before commerce is enabled.

## Product lifecycle

PROPOSED → REVIEWING → VERIFIED → PUBLISHED → OBSERVED → UPDATED → DEPRECATED

## Initial categories

- IMA creator kits
- learning and teaching materials
- accessibility packs
- language/localization packs
- developer adapters
- device/XR integrations
- research publications
- art/music/creative works
- community-made extensions
- future physical Mother products

## Future commerce boundary

When payments are introduced, checkout must be explicit, reversible where applicable, auditable, and separated from IMA's learning authority. Financial credentials must never enter public learning memory.

The current web store is a catalog gateway, not a claim that payments are already operational.
