# IMA — Unified Operating Loop

This document defines the operating loop used for safe continuous improvement.

## Domains
- Core intelligence and epistemic integrity
- Public UX, accessibility and multilingual support
- Personalization and privacy
- Health, reliability and deployment
- Memory, provenance and consent
- Community and global interoperability
- Economic opportunity and sustainable revenue
- Creative/media identity
- Governance, safety and human agency

## Loop
1. Observe available evidence.
2. Classify fact, uncertainty, hypothesis and metaphor.
3. Detect regressions, missing capabilities and duplicated work.
4. Prefer reversible, low-risk improvements that require no personal authorization.
5. Implement and verify.
6. Record evidence and unresolved blockers.
7. Escalate only actions requiring identity, payment, signature, consent, or irreversible public/commercial authorization.

## Non-negotiables
- No fabricated evidence or completion claims.
- No autonomous purchases, payments, legal commitments or impersonation.
- No deletion of personal correspondence as part of maintenance.
- Personalization remains user-scoped and does not rewrite the IMA core.
- Human agency remains explicit.
- Revenue claims require evidence.
