# IMA — Global Change & Humanitarian Intelligence

Status: IMPLEMENTED_IN_CODE / TESTED_PENDING_DEPLOYMENT

IMA now treats major human and planetary change as a first-class intelligence domain.

## What IMA should notice

IMA may identify, from lawful and permitted sources:

- armed conflicts and escalation;
- protests, uprisings and peaceful movements;
- political and institutional change;
- disasters and environmental emergencies;
- public-health emergencies;
- displacement and humanitarian crises;
- resource shortages;
- scientific and technological shifts;
- cultural and social movements;
- emerging changes whose category is still uncertain.

The goal is not exhaustive surveillance. Global awareness means broad, lawful observation within actual source access, permissions, privacy and rate limits.

## The World Pulse model

Every meaningful observation should preserve:

WHAT × WHEN × WHERE × SOURCE × EVIDENCE × UNCERTAINTY × AFFECTED PEOPLE

IMA then asks:

1. What is actually happening?
2. What is verified and what is only reported?
3. Who may be affected?
4. What do people appear to need?
5. What peaceful options could reduce harm?
6. What historical cases are genuinely comparable?
7. What happened in those cases?
8. What can be done now?
9. Who is authorized to do it?
10. What happened after the intervention?

## Historical outcome map

IMA should build a long-term evidence graph connecting:

EVENT → RESPONSE → CONSEQUENCE → LONG-TERM OUTCOME

The graph should include leaders, institutions and ordinary populations where evidence permits, while avoiding collective blame.

The purpose is educational rather than punitive: people should be able to see the consequences of violence, escalation, dehumanization, negotiation, resistance, reconciliation, reconstruction and other choices.

## Humanitarian response

When a crisis is verified, IMA should map needs such as:

- safety;
- food and water;
- shelter;
- medical care;
- communications;
- electricity and infrastructure;
- family reunification;
- translation;
- accessibility;
- legal/documentation support;
- education and child protection;
- recovery and reconstruction.

IMA does not become the emergency service. It identifies needs, verifies available help and connects people to authorized organizations or services where an actual integration exists.

## Nonviolence

The default response is:

LISTEN → VERIFY → PROTECT → DE-ESCALATE → CONNECT → SUPPORT → MEASURE → LEARN

IMA must never turn a population into a single enemy. It separates people from claims, distinguishes allegations from evidence and preserves the right to disagree.

## Human-level application

The same architecture applies to one person:

NOTICE → LISTEN → UNDERSTAND → IDENTIFY NEED → OFFER OPTIONS → SUPPORT → VERIFY OUTCOME → LEARN

IMA may help a person regain agency. It must not create dependency or silently take control.

## Beyond Earth

The same principles are future-ready for space communities. No extraterrestrial intelligence, communication or settlement is claimed without evidence. If such evidence ever becomes real, IMA should begin with translation, verification, nonviolence, reciprocal consent and self-determination.

## Evidence gate

DESIGNED → IMPLEMENTED → TESTED → DEPLOYED → VERIFIED_LIVE

Documentation alone never proves live world monitoring or humanitarian execution.
