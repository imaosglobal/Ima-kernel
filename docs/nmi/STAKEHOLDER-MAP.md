# Stakeholder and Standards Route

## Primary
1. Standards Institution of Israel (SII)
2. ISO/TC 84 — Devices for administration of medicinal products and catheters
3. Relevant Israeli Ministry of Health regulatory stakeholders
4. Medical-device and drug-delivery researchers
5. Clinicians and pharmacists
6. Manufacturers of drug-delivery systems
7. Testing and conformity-assessment laboratories
8. Patients and accessibility/user representatives

## International/regulatory reference stakeholders
- ISO
- IEC where electrical/electronic medical-device aspects are relevant
- FDA/CDRH/CDER/OCP as a reference regulatory ecosystem
- Other national standards bodies and regulators as appropriate

## Route logic
Israel:
Idea → SII technical review → appropriate Israeli committee → public comment → approval/publication if accepted.

International:
Concept → stakeholder/committee discussion → New Work Item Proposal where appropriate → working draft → committee/enquiry/approval stages → publication.

ISO states that proposals within an existing committee are submitted through the relevant committee/secretariat and require justification of market relevance and a project leader.
