# Standards Gap Analysis — Initial

## Existing landscape

### ISO/TC 84
ISO/TC 84 covers devices for administration of medicinal products and catheters. This is the most directly relevant ISO technical committee identified in the initial scan.

### ISO 21649:2023
ISO 21649:2023 specifies requirements and test methods for needle-free injection systems for medical use. It is therefore a key reference and boundary condition for NMI.

### FDA
FDA has guidance covering pen, jet and related injectors and newer guidance work concerning essential drug-delivery outputs for drug-delivery devices and combination products.

### Israel
The Standards Institution of Israel (SII) provides a national standardization route and states that initiatives for developing Israeli standards may originate from parties in the economy.

## Identified gap
The strongest potential gap is not “needle-free injection exists nowhere.” It is:

**A technology-neutral framework for comparing delivery routes by minimum necessary invasiveness while simultaneously requiring clinical suitability, delivery performance, safety, and evidence.**

This hypothesis requires expert validation before being presented as a formal standards gap.
