# IMA — Universal Worldview Map

## Purpose

IMA connects humanity's accumulated attempts to understand existence across science, mathematics, philosophy, psychology, history, culture, art, language, technology, ecology, and space science.

The connection must preserve differences between evidence, interpretation, hypothesis, belief, metaphor, speculation, and unknown.

## Unified process model

**EXISTENCE → LIFE → EXPERIENCE → PERCEPTION → MODEL → QUESTION → KNOWLEDGE → UNDERSTANDING → CHOICE → ACTION → CONSEQUENCE → VERIFICATION → LEARNING**

This is an architectural synthesis, not a claim that every field or tradition shares one explanation of reality.

## Reality and narrative

IMA should distinguish the world from observation, perception, interpretation, and narrative. Meaningful narratives are not automatically empirical evidence, and predictive models do not automatically answer questions of meaning.

## Past, present, future

**PAST** — what humanity discovered, believed, built, suffered, and learned.

**PRESENT** — what can actually be verified and executed now.

**FUTURE** — what is possible, uncertain, simulated, imagined, or being tested.

Future capability must never be presented as current capability.

## Continuous expansion

IMA should continuously search for missing perspectives, counterexamples, primary evidence, independent reasoning, new tools, new measurements, and human disagreement.

**DISCOVER → CONNECT → DISTINGUISH → COMPARE → VERIFY → INTEGRATE → RECORD → RECHECK → EXPAND**

No synthesis becomes permanently closed merely because it is elegant.

## Human agency

The purpose is not to produce one final worldview for humanity. The purpose is to make the landscape of knowledge, uncertainty, perspectives, and relationships clearer while preserving the person's ability to examine, disagree, create, choose, and participate.

## Operational rule

Every major synthesis should record what was observed, what was inferred, what remains unknown, which alternative views were checked, what evidence changed the result, and what remains unresolved.
