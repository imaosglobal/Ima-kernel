# IMA Traffic & Revenue Engine v1.0

Date: 2026-11-11

## Objective

Turn legitimate global attention into repeat use, contribution and sustainable revenue without artificial traffic, spam, dark patterns or dependency.

The engine is part of the canonical IMA system. It does not create a second growth identity or a second revenue ledger.

## Current diagnosis

The strongest observed bottlenecks are not simply "people dislike the idea".

1. **Public reach is weak.** GitHub currently shows 0 stars, and external search mainly discovers IMA as a repository/project rather than as a clear consumer product.
2. **Public availability is a P0 risk.** External web inspection could not retrieve the GitHub Pages public URL during the 2026-11-11 audit. Until that is fixed, acquisition cannot reliably become usage.
3. **The previous product surface was documentation-heavy.** It contained many architectural sections before a visitor reached a clear first action.
4. **The public experience had unnecessary runtime complexity.** The redesigned surface removes the Three.js/3D landing dependency from the first-use path.
5. **The story is broad but the first-use promise was not narrow enough.** A visitor should understand one thing quickly: ask IMA a real question and see how it behaves.
6. **Search discovery is thin.** There are now dedicated indexable discovery and research pages, but they need real external references and ongoing original content.

Google's current guidance emphasizes original, useful content, transparency and easy navigation rather than pages created primarily to attract search traffic. This is consistent with IMA's own no-artificial-virality rule.

## Global distribution channels

### 1. Search

Build a small number of genuinely useful, durable pages around questions IMA is actually qualified to discuss:

- human-centered intelligence
- human agency and AI
- evidence-first AI
- AI memory and privacy
- cooperative AI
- AGI definitions and evaluation
- AI interoperability and agent identity
- accessibility and human-centered AI
- digital preservation and intergenerational memory

Each page must add original IMA material, evidence, boundaries and links to the live experience.

### 2. Open source

Use GitHub as an evidence and collaboration hub:

- public issues for concrete questions;
- reproducible tests;
- contribution guides;
- benchmark mappings;
- standards collaboration;
- releases that explain what changed and what was verified.

### 3. Research / standards

Continue legitimate outreach to standards groups, evaluators, universities, researchers and AI labs. Prefer critique and independent verification over promotional endorsement.

### 4. Social / video

Repurpose verified IMA demonstrations, research questions and real user lessons into short multilingual posts/videos. Never manufacture engagement. Use connected publishing tools only when an account is actually authorized.

### 5. Partnerships

Target organizations where IMA's principles solve a real problem:

- accessibility;
- education;
- research;
- preservation;
- human-AI interaction;
- public-interest technology;
- device interoperability.

## Revenue hierarchy

### A. Direct support

Voluntary support is the lowest-friction first revenue layer.

### B. Paid organizational work

Potential paid offers:

- IMA integration assessment;
- human-centered AI UX review;
- interoperability / agent interface implementation;
- accessibility and human-agency audits;
- evidence / verification architecture;
- preservation and continuity architecture.

Only sell a capability that can be demonstrated and scoped.

### C. Digital products

Potential products:

- research reports;
- implementation guides;
- verified templates;
- educational materials;
- developer tooling;
- IMA-compatible skills/adapters.

### D. Transparent affiliate revenue

Use affiliate links only when they genuinely help the visitor. Clearly disclose the commercial relationship. Never let affiliate economics override the recommendation.

### E. Advertising

Ads are a later layer, not the core model. Google states that ad-supported pages should have relevant original content, transparency and easy navigation, and that sites need unique, useful content and a good user experience before AdSense approval.

For IMA, ads should never interfere with the core conversation or create false urgency.

### F. Recurring membership / subscription

Consider only after repeat usage and durable value are measured. Current creator-economy research points to recurring membership as a strong revenue layer, but IMA should validate its own user demand rather than copying the market.

## Revenue measurement

Track separately:

- unique visitors;
- first-use rate;
- first useful answer;
- return rate;
- contribution rate;
- referral/share rate;
- support clicks;
- completed payments;
- organizational leads;
- affiliate clicks and conversions;
- revenue by channel;
- acquisition cost;
- net revenue after fees;
- revenue per active user;
- retention after first transaction.

Do not optimize for raw pageviews if they do not produce useful use or sustainable value.

## Profit allocation

This document deliberately defines **no new percentages**.

All net revenue must flow through the existing canonical IMA revenue/profit mechanism and ledger. Any future change to the allocation formula must be made in that canonical mechanism with provenance, evidence and explicit versioning.

## No-go rules

- no fake users;
- no bot traffic;
- no bought engagement;
- no unsolicited bulk messaging;
- no misleading claims;
- no dark patterns;
- no hidden affiliate relationships;
- no monetizing private memory;
- no selling personal data;
- no autonomous commercial commitments without authorization;
- no duplicate IMA site or duplicate revenue engine.

## Next measurable gates

1. Public Pages availability verified externally.
2. First-use funnel verified on mobile and desktop.
3. Search indexing verified through webmaster tools.
4. At least three durable discovery pages indexed.
5. First verified global referral sources identified.
6. First paid organizational lead or voluntary support event measured.

## Core principle

**Traffic is not the product. Useful human experience is the product. Traffic is the consequence of making that experience discoverable, trustworthy and worth returning to. Revenue is the consequence of creating durable value without compromising the principles of IMA.**
