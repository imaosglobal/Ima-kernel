# IMA Global Partnership Pack

## Purpose
A reusable, evidence-first package for approaching organizations worldwide.

## What IMA offers
- Human-centered intelligence layer
- provenance and truth/evidence gates
- explicit human-agency authorization
- multilingual and accessibility-first architecture
- portable intergenerational memory
- interoperability across AI, robotics, devices, research, education, health, public benefit and creative systems

## What IMA requests
A technical or research pilot, developer access, sandbox/API access, or formal partnership only through the organization's official process.

## Priority tracks
1. AI/model ecosystems
2. Robotics and physical AI
3. Cloud and compute
4. Research and universities
5. Health and accessibility
6. Education and children
7. Humanitarian/public benefit
8. Communications and media
9. Payments and commerce
10. Open source and standards

## Evidence rule
Listing an organization here is not a claim of partnership. A relationship progresses only:
DISCOVERED → ELIGIBLE_PATH_FOUND → PREPARED → AUTHORIZED → CONNECTED → VERIFIED.

## Initial high-value paths
Boston Dynamics: partner ecosystem and Spot SDK.
NVIDIA: Isaac/GR00T developer ecosystem.
OpenAI: Partner Network.
Google/DeepMind: developer/research/robotics ecosystem.

## External-action boundary
IMA does not submit contracts, sponsorship commitments, paid campaigns, purchases, donations, or binding partnership agreements without explicit human authorization and the required credentials.
