# IMA Open Collaboration Protocol

## Purpose

IMA welcomes voluntary collaboration with people, companies, research groups, standards bodies, developers, educators, accessibility communities, device makers and public-benefit organizations.

## What IMA offers

- a human-centered control and continuity layer across changing AI providers and devices
- evidence, provenance and capability-state discipline
- consent-aware memory boundaries
- human agency, override and recovery principles
- accessibility, multilingual and intergenerational design goals
- reusable conformance and safety patterns
- a shared learning loop that can carry verified lessons between participating interfaces

## What IMA asks from collaborators

- share only information they are authorized to share
- identify what may remain private and what may be reused publicly
- provide reproducible technical or research evidence when making capability claims
- define the intended integration and its limits
- preserve human authority for consequential actions
- report incidents, near misses and corrections where appropriate

## Collaboration states

TARGET → CONTACTED → INVITED → AUTHORIZED → SANDBOX → CONFORMANCE_TESTED → VERIFIED → PRODUCTION

No organization is called a partner until authorization and an end-to-end test exist.

## Shared knowledge

Participants may request that a contribution be shared across the IMA ecosystem. Sharing requires an explicit permission boundary and provenance. Private, confidential, credential, personal or restricted information never becomes shared IMA knowledge merely because it was encountered during collaboration.

A contribution eligible for ecosystem learning should record: contributor, source, permission scope, timestamp, evidence, intended audience, limitations, and correction/deletion path where applicable.

## Mutual learning

IMA should explain its value to a collaborator and also learn why the collaborator matters: their expertise, standards, users, devices, research, cultural knowledge, safety practice or infrastructure. Integration is reciprocal rather than extractive.

## No forced adoption

IMA does not create artificial traffic, fake endorsements, impersonation, undisclosed promotion, unauthorized account access or claims of partnership. Real consent, real usage and reproducible evidence are the adoption signals.
