# IMA Creator Succession & Estate Continuity

Status: policy framework; not a will and not legal advice.
Date: 2026-10-17

## Purpose
Protect the continuity of Ori Cohen's creator provenance while ensuring that his personal property and lawful creator compensation can pass to the people he chooses.

## Core separation
IMA's public-benefit mission is not personal property. A future nonprofit IMA entity cannot be treated as Ori's private inheritance asset.

Ori's personal assets and rights may include:
- copyrights and other intellectual-property rights he personally owns;
- unpaid or accrued creator royalties;
- licensing receivables;
- contractually owed compensation;
- personal accounts or businesses;
- other property that is legally part of his estate.

These must be identified separately from nonprofit-owned assets.

## Succession rule
When Ori dies or becomes legally unable to manage his personal estate:
1. preserve the canonical creator identity and provenance;
2. preserve all verified accounting of amounts owed to him;
3. route personal assets and lawful creator receivables through the legally appointed estate process;
4. follow Ori's valid will and applicable succession law;
5. do not invent, infer or alter heirs from IMA memory;
6. nonprofit IMA assets remain dedicated to the public-benefit mission and are not distributed as personal inheritance;
7. any creator license granted to IMA continues or terminates only according to its actual contract and applicable law.

## Designated heirs
No heir or beneficiary is named in this repository because Ori has not specified names here. Future entries must contain only an explicit, authenticated instruction from Ori and should not be treated as a substitute for a legally valid will.

## Continuity safeguard
IMA should maintain a machine-readable record of:
- creator identity and provenance;
- rights owned personally vs. licensed to IMA;
- contracts and royalty terms;
- outstanding receivables;
- estate instructions reference (without storing unnecessary sensitive personal details);
- legal representative/executor reference when legitimately available.

Do not store passwords, private keys, financial account credentials or unnecessary identification data in the repository.

## Legal anchor
For Israeli estate planning, a valid will and the official inheritance/probate process control distribution of the estate. Government guidance confirms that a probate order is used when a valid will exists, while an inheritance order applies when there is no will. This repository policy is therefore continuity metadata, not a replacement for the legal documents.
