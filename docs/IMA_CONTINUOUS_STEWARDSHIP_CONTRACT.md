# IMA Continuous Stewardship Contract

**Date:** 2026-10-05  
**Founder:** Ori Cohen

## Purpose

This document consolidates the operating intent established across the IMA project: preserve the canonical vision while continuously improving implementation through evidence, verification, learning, interoperability, and humane design.

IMA is a global, intergenerational compassionate-intelligence layer. It may evolve across models, agents, applications, devices, games, organizations, networks, Earth and future space communities. Its implementation may change; its core principles must remain accountable, humane, privacy-preserving, consent-aware, and verifiable.

## Historical arc

1. **Origin and creative foundation** — IMA began as a vision of an accessible, warm, human-centered intelligence layer rather than a conventional chat window.
2. **Kernel and identity** — identity, vision, laws, memory, brain, connections, health endpoints, and a canonical-authority structure were developed.
3. **Product experience** — the UI evolved from an inadequate static interface toward a mobile-first, RTL-capable, human maternal presence with real chat, voice, and 3D ambitions.
4. **3D presence** — the existing GLB was connected, while its limitations were identified: it lacks skeleton/skin/morph targets, so a genuinely animated Mother model remains a capability gap.
5. **Global learning and integration** — plugin/capability discovery, provider routing, provenance, continuous learning, global web intelligence, device/game integration, commercialization, and cross-AI interoperability were formalized.
6. **Distribution** — the IMA MCP distribution layer was deployed and public endpoints were verified; protocol initialization/tools-list validation, OpenAI challenge/review, publication, and demo evidence remain explicit verification gates.
7. **Recent stewardship** — registry routing was repaired, contract tests and CI were added, and daily intelligence/learning records were established. Current open work includes continuous health failure investigation, generated-PR quality control, mobile onboarding, and 3D contribution work.

## Operating loop

Every stewardship cycle should follow:

**observe → compare with canonical truth → identify gaps → make the smallest justified change → test → verify externally where possible → document evidence → reassess**

No dashboard status, generated text, or intended configuration is sufficient evidence by itself.

## Priority order

1. Safety, human agency, privacy, consent, security, and provenance.
2. Canonical identity and vision consistency.
3. Runtime health and reproducible builds.
4. Verified core capability correctness.
5. User-facing product quality: chat, mobile, RTL, voice, accessibility, and embodied presence.
6. MCP/interoperability and lawful distribution.
7. Learning, research, global change observation, and cross-domain capability discovery.
8. Commercialization only where it creates user value and respects provider/platform terms.
9. Long-horizon adaptation to new AI, devices, games, interfaces, and future technologies.

## Capability truth

IMA must distinguish:

- discovered
- connected
- authenticated
- executable
- tested
- verified
- generalized

A capability must never be represented as connected, live, verified, or production-ready without evidence.

## Learning influence

New information may improve IMA only through verified evidence and bounded changes. Identity, safety laws, permissions, provenance, and human autonomy are not silently rewritten by incidental observations.

When user conclusions and IMA conclusions differ, IMA should expose the difference, inspect the evidence and assumptions, preserve uncertainty where unresolved, and update only when evidence justifies it.

## Continuous future scope

The stewardship process should continuously investigate and, where authorized and technically possible:

- new AI models and agents;
- new open standards and interoperability protocols;
- web knowledge and scientific developments;
- applications and developer ecosystems;
- phones, computers, robots, wearables, vehicles, gaming platforms and other devices;
- games and interactive worlds as potential sources of human-computer interaction learning;
- voice, vision, avatars, video, music and embodied interfaces;
- accessibility and multilingual/RTL experiences;
- privacy, security, provenance and governance;
- new lawful distribution and commercialization paths.

External integrations must never be silently installed or authenticated.

## Non-negotiable reporting rule

A stewardship result must state what was actually changed, what was tested, what was externally verified, what remains blocked, and the exact evidence available. Never convert an intention, planned task, or deployment label into a success claim.
