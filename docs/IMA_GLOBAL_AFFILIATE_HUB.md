# IMA Global Affiliate Hub

IMA Global Affiliate Hub is the affiliate/referral surface embedded in the IMA user experience.

## Current integration

- UI: `ima-ui/src/App.jsx`
- Styling: `ima-ui/src/index.css`
- Navigation: `#affiliate`
- Provider: Affiracle
- Program: AliExpress Affiliate
- Referral: https://affiracle.com/he/aliexpress.html?AFFID=AFF9334
- Earnings: https://affiracle.com/affiliates/aliexpress/earnings

## Verified program information

Affiracle states that AliExpress commissions vary, with current program information describing average commissions around 8%–12% and advertising commissions up to 16%. Affiracle also states that a referrer receives 5% of the AliExpress commission earned by a referred sub-affiliate, without deducting that 5% from the sub-affiliate.

## Rules

1. Never promise a specific income.
2. Clearly disclose possible referral commissions.
3. Do not present unverified integrations as active.
4. Do not spam, scrape contact lists, or send unsolicited outreach.
5. Keep human control over publishing and outreach.
6. Treat commission rates and provider terms as changeable external facts.
7. Prefer useful educational content over repeated promotion.

## Architecture

IMA -> Global Affiliate Hub -> Affiracle -> AliExpress Affiliate Program

The Hub is an IMA distribution/opportunity layer. It does not imply that IMA owns, operates, or represents Affiracle or AliExpress.
