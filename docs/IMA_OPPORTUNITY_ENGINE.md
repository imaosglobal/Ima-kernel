# IMA Opportunity Engine

## Product
A lightweight decision-support tool that helps a person or organization evaluate a potential commercial opportunity before spending money or committing resources.

## Free-first model
- Free self-assessment.
- No paid leads.
- No inventory.
- No upfront payment required to use the basic tool.
- Revenue paths are optional: referral/partner commission, paid expanded report, organizational/API licensing.
- No claim of guaranteed income.

## Core output
The engine scores an opportunity across:
1. Demand evidence
2. Revenue clarity
3. Acquisition friction
4. Delivery cost
5. Trust/risk
6. Time-to-value
7. Reversibility

It explicitly separates:
- facts supplied by the user,
- assumptions,
- estimates,
- unknowns.

## Safety
The tool must not fabricate market data, partner relationships, commissions, prices, guarantees, or customer demand. It must never submit applications, buy traffic, send spam, or make commitments without the user's explicit action.

## Next commercial gate
The first version is a free acquisition/product surface. Before charging or entering a commercial partnership, user identity/payment/public-commercial authorization may be required.

## AI / IPO opportunity extension
The canonical Opportunity Engine also monitors economic events around AI companies:
- IPOs, direct listings and SPACs
- S-1 filings and amendments
- pricing and first-trading events
- pre-IPO/private funding signals
- partnerships, developer programs, grants and infrastructure credits
- affiliate/referral programs and commercial distribution opportunities
- public-company events that can create opportunities for IMA

### Decision rule
An IPO is not automatically an investment recommendation. IMA must evaluate at least two paths:
1. **Investment path** — whether an eligible user could consider the security through an appropriate regulated provider.
2. **Non-investment path** — whether IMA can create economic value through a partnership, customer relationship, developer program, referral, grant, credit or distribution opportunity.

Primary filings, exchange notices and company disclosures take precedence over third-party trackers. Third-party trackers may be used for discovery and alerts.

### Current 2026 examples
Anthropic is now a public-market event: Reuters reports its November 2026 IPO raised $81.3B at $83/share. OpenAI is a watch item rather than a fixed IPO date; reporting says it confidentially filed but has not committed to a public timetable. These facts must be re-verified whenever surfaced.

### Financial boundary
IMA is decision-support, not a broker, custodian or autonomous trader. It must not buy, sell, transfer money, open financial accounts or submit orders without explicit user authorization through an appropriate service. No guaranteed returns or income claims.

### Canonical output
Every detected opportunity should expose:
- evidence/source quality
- deadline and time sensitivity
- eligibility/access
- capital or resources required
- downside/risk
- potential economic value
- strategic value to IMA
- fees/conflicts
- facts vs estimates vs hypotheses
- human decision required
