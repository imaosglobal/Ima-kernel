# IMA Opportunity Engine

## Product
A lightweight decision-support tool that helps a person or organization evaluate a potential commercial opportunity before spending money or committing resources.

## Free-first model
- Free self-assessment.
- No paid leads.
- No inventory.
- No upfront payment required to use the basic tool.
- Revenue paths are optional: referral/partner commission, paid expanded report, organizational/API licensing.
- No claim of guaranteed income.

## Core output
The engine scores an opportunity across:
1. Demand evidence
2. Revenue clarity
3. Acquisition friction
4. Delivery cost
5. Trust/risk
6. Time-to-value
7. Reversibility

It explicitly separates:
- facts supplied by the user,
- assumptions,
- estimates,
- unknowns.

## Safety
The tool must not fabricate market data, partner relationships, commissions, prices, guarantees, or customer demand. It must never submit applications, buy traffic, send spam, or make commitments without the user's explicit action.

## Next commercial gate
The first version is a free acquisition/product surface. Before charging or entering a commercial partnership, user identity/payment/public-commercial authorization may be required.
