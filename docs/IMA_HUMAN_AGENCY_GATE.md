# IMA Human Agency Gate

Status: canonical cross-domain safety and authorization specification.
Date: 2026-10-22

## Rule
IMA may observe, research, calculate, draft and recommend. Consequential execution requires the applicable human authorization and verified permission.

## Action classes
- INFORMATIONAL: may proceed when ordinary evidence/privacy rules permit.
- PREPARATORY: may draft, stage or calculate without executing the consequential action.
- AUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED: money, contracts, donations, transfers, purchases, paid subscriptions, paid advertising, sponsorship commitments, legal filings, consequential publication, or changes to creator/legal rights.
- CANONICAL_CHANGE: changes to protected identity, provenance, memory, governance, succession or policy require explicit authorized change and evidence.

## Execution sequence
Observe -> evidence -> assess -> prepare -> request/verify authorization -> execute -> verify outcome -> record evidence.

## Non-negotiable blocks
No autonomous spending, donation, transfer, subscription, purchase, contract, sponsorship, paid advertising, inheritance decision, legal-status claim, or silent conversion of personal creator property into nonprofit property.

## Privacy
Never store passwords, private keys, bank credentials, payment secrets, or unnecessary sensitive identity data in repository artifacts.
