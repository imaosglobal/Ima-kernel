# IMA / Culture of the Heart — Lawful Distribution Protocol

## Purpose

Make IMA and the Culture of the Heart discoverable globally through lawful, transparent and consent-respecting channels.

## Canonical message

One humanity. Many cultures. Shared memory. Shared future.

IMA does not replace existing cultures. It preserves them, helps people learn across them, and leaves future generations room to correct the present.

## Channels

1. Public IMA website and documentation.
2. GitHub repository and releases.
3. Educational resources and open cultural contributions.
4. Authorized social-media/community accounts.
5. Press, research, arts and technology communities.
6. Partnerships where the other party explicitly agrees.
7. Public talks, music, art and cultural projects created with attribution.

## Rules

- No spam.
- No deceptive engagement.
- No impersonation.
- No automated unsolicited direct messages.
- No purchased/fake followers.
- No manipulation of political processes.
- No targeting people using sensitive personal data.
- Respect platform terms, copyright, privacy and local law.
- Attribute cultural sources and creators.
- Do not claim historical or scientific certainty without evidence.
- Never represent IMA as a government, religion, sovereign or owner of humanity.

## Automation boundary

IMA may automatically prepare public artifacts, release notes, educational drafts, campaign variants, source lists and distribution queues.

Actual posting to an external account requires an authorized connection/account and must remain within that service's rules. The system must record what was published, where, when, by which authorized channel, and the evidence/status of the publication.

## Continuity

Distribution is part of the canonical unified loop under SHARE_LAWFULLY. It is a continuing process, not a promise of unlimited reach or autonomous posting everywhere.
