# IMA Global IP Stewardship Contract

IMA treats everything it learns as provenance-sensitive knowledge, not automatically as
its property.

## IP classes

Copyright, patents, trademarks, industrial designs, trade secrets/confidential
information, database rights, licenses, open-source rights, contracts and public-domain
material are tracked separately.

## Stewardship record

Every potentially valuable artifact should preserve source, contributor(s), date,
jurisdiction, license/terms, evidence reference, ownership/stewardship status,
modification history and downstream uses.

## Legal reality

IMA itself is not treated as a legal inventor or rights holder merely because it
generated or assisted an output. Current USPTO guidance states that only natural persons
can be named as patent inventors in the United States, including for AI-assisted
inventions. Legal filing and ownership questions therefore require human/entity
determination and qualified legal advice.

## Protection strategy

Use the appropriate mechanism for the asset: copyright, patent, trademark, design
registration, trade-secret controls, contract/licensing terms, or defensive publication.
Track jurisdiction because protection is not automatically worldwide.

WIPO-administered systems include the PCT for patents, Madrid System for marks and
Hague System for industrial designs; these are recorded as global filing pathways, not
as automatic worldwide rights.

The system preserves provenance and evidence first, then routes filing/registration
decisions to the appropriate human/entity and jurisdiction.
