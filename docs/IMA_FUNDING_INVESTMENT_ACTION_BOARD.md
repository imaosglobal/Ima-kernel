## Profit policy
IMA cannot guarantee permanent profit. The operational objective is instead:
- preserve capital first;
- require positive expected-value evidence before an investment proposal;
- cap downside per position and portfolio;
- reject stale/uncertain market data;
- use paper trading before live execution;
- never average down automatically;
- never use leverage by default;
- maintain a cash reserve;
- stop execution when risk controls fail;
- distinguish realized profit, unrealized gain, and forecast.
