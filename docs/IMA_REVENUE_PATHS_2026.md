# IMA Revenue Paths — 2026

## Objective

Generate legitimate revenue for IMA before creating recurring paid AI/API expenditure.

This is a commercial operating plan, not a claim that IMA currently has revenue.

## Priority 1 — paid organizational pilots

Offer organizations a bounded, human-approved pilot using capabilities that already exist in IMA:

- AI/agent QA and evidence verification
- human-centered AI evaluation
- accessibility and multilingual review
- AI workflow / browser automation architecture review
- knowledge-preservation and provenance architecture

Delivery is scoped, priced and invoiced only after a customer accepts a written offer.

## Priority 2 — sponsorship and project funding

IMA is an open-source project. Potential funding routes include:

- project sponsorship
- recurring individual support
- company sponsorship
- research sponsorship
- grants and public innovation programs

A funding platform is used only when its legal/tax arrangement is appropriate for the recipient.

## Priority 3 — grants

The Israeli Innovation Authority currently lists the Tnufa track for Israeli individual entrepreneurs or eligible companies developing innovative technology. The published program states that an individual entrepreneur may apply and that incorporation can occur later after committee approval.

A grant is funding, not sales revenue, and must never be represented as revenue until actually awarded and received.

## Priority 4 — transparent commercial partnerships

Potential partners can sponsor a clearly identified IMA capability or pay for an integration/service. No sponsor may alter IMA's factual standards, privacy rules, safety rules or human-agency principles.

## Revenue-before-spend gate

Paid AI/API usage remains blocked unless:

1. verified revenue exists, or
2. a human explicitly authorizes a separate budget.

No automatic payment, card addition, credit purchase or budget increase is permitted.

## Accounting and legal gate

Before accepting business income personally or through IMA, the receiving legal/tax structure must be appropriate.

For an Israeli individual business, the Israel Tax Authority provides online registration for eligible exempt dealers; the published 2026 turnover ceiling is 122,833 NIS, subject to the applicable rules and occupation exclusions.

This document is not tax or legal advice. The actual structure must be confirmed with the Israel Tax Authority/accountant before collecting taxable business income.

## Current funnel

Discover IMA
-> request sponsorship / pilot
-> human review
-> written scope
-> legal/tax/payment check
-> payment or grant receipt
-> record verified revenue
-> allocate a controlled reserve
-> only then enable paid AI usage.

## Non-permitted monetization

- deceptive advertising
- undisclosed affiliate relationships
- selling private user data
- charging for capabilities that are not actually available
- autonomous purchasing
- fabricated revenue claims
- pay-to-alter factual or safety conclusions
