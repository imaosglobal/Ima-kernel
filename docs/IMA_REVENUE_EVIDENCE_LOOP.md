# IMA Revenue Evidence Loop

Status: canonical Creator-to-Commerce extension of the unified daily IMA Global Health Loop.
Date: 2026-10-22

## Pipeline
Verified publication -> reach -> views -> engagement -> clicks -> conversions -> confirmed revenue.

Metrics are observations, not income. Revenue enters the ledger only when actual receipt or contractually committed receivable is verified with evidence under the financial governance rules.

## Required revenue evidence
- event identifier and timestamp
- source/program/provider
- media or campaign version
- country/availability and eligibility evidence when relevant
- gross amount and original currency
- transaction/platform fees
- taxes
- net amount
- received/payout status
- evidence reference
- attribution basis
- authorization where applicable

## Monetization priority
Prefer lawful, free/no-upfront-cost routes first. Verify eligibility, country availability, payout terms, taxes and enrollment before presenting a route as available.

## Language rule
Expected, projected, eligible, estimated or potential money is never labeled income/revenue received.

## Safety
The loop prepares and reports. It does not autonomously spend, donate, transfer, subscribe, advertise, contract, sponsor or purchase.
