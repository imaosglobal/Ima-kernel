# IMA Impact Window — Invention Disclosure

Inventor attribution: Ori Cohen

Working name: IMA Impact Window — חלון ההשפעה

Technical family: Personal Interactive Intelligence Window (PIIW)

## Core invention concept
A user-scoped interactive intelligence space that can persist across contexts and devices, observe authorized context, obtain authorized specialist intelligence, perform authorized actions, verify the resulting state, and maintain an auditable chronological timeline of the user's interaction with the window.

## The intended transformation
The window is not merely an overlay. It is a persistent personal intelligence space whose purpose is to increase a person's ability to understand, decide, create and act while reducing dependence on any single AI provider or interface.

The system can adapt its surface to the host: web window, split view, detached window, native overlay, compact/PiP surface, external display or future spatial interface. The logical identity and continuity remain the same.

## Key technical combination to investigate for protection
1. user-scoped persistent interactive intelligence window;
2. host-independent capability negotiation;
3. authorized observation of the current user context;
4. provider-neutral federation of multiple intelligences through IMA;
5. explicit authorization before context transfer or action;
6. authorized action execution;
7. post-action verification against observed state;
8. chronological per-user event timeline linking request, observation, authorization, action, result and verification;
9. privacy-preserving provenance and minimum-necessary context;
10. cross-device/window continuity;
11. learning and improvement from verified outcomes rather than unverified claims;
12. capability adapters that allow the same logical window to evolve as operating systems, devices and intelligence providers evolve.

## Higher-impact research direction
The strongest long-term version is a universal agency layer: the system should help a person become more capable rather than more dependent. A user can move from one device, application, intelligence or modality to another while retaining authorized continuity, evidence and control.

A Nobel-level aspiration is not a legal or scientific claim. The measurable target is global human benefit: improved accessibility, learning, problem solving, creativity, safety and agency, with reproducible evidence across languages, cultures, ages and devices.

## Patent strategy boundary

A first patent filing can establish a priority date; a PCT application can generally be filed within 12 months of the earliest priority filing. Patentability, ownership and enforceability require professional legal analysis.
