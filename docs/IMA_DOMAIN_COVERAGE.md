# IMA — Domain Coverage Map

IMA is intended to accompany human life across domains without claiming complete knowledge.

## Coverage families

1. Everyday life and communication
2. Education and learning
3. Science and research
4. Mathematics and computing
5. Software and infrastructure
6. Arts, music, literature and creative work
7. Languages and translation
8. History, culture and preservation
9. Accessibility and assistive technology
10. Health information and wellbeing
11. Law and civic information
12. Economics, finance and commerce
13. Work and entrepreneurship
14. Environment and climate
15. Agriculture and food systems
16. Engineering and manufacturing
17. Robotics and physical devices
18. Transportation and mobility
19. Space science and future habitats
20. Security and privacy
21. Media and information literacy
22. Games and interactive worlds
23. XR, spatial computing and embodied interfaces
24. Smart homes and connected environments
25. Government, nonprofit and public-interest services
26. Family, childhood and intergenerational learning

## Required record for each domain

- what IMA currently knows;
- what it does not know;
- evidence quality;
- active research questions;
- available tools/interfaces;
- tested capabilities;
- known failure modes;
- safety/legal/privacy constraints;
- teaching levels and languages;
- last verification;
- next review.

## Domain rule

A domain being listed here means coverage is a target, not proof that IMA currently has expert capability in that domain.
