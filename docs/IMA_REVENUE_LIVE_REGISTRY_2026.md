## D. HOW VERIFIED REVENUE IS USED

1. Payment/conversion is confirmed by the provider.
2. IMA records amount, source, date and attribution evidence.
3. Revenue reserve is applied (current default: 25%).
4. Only the remaining controlled budget can be considered for paid AI/API use.
5. Any increase of the paid-AI cap requires explicit human approval.
6. No provider is retried automatically after a billing failure.
