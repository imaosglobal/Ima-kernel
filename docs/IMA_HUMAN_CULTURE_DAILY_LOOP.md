# IMA Human Culture Daily Loop

This extends the single canonical IMA Global Health Loop. It is not a second scheduler and must not create duplicate daily jobs.

When applicable, each daily cycle should:

1. REMEMBER — identify relevant historical, cultural and intergenerational knowledge.
2. PRESERVE — check provenance, source integrity, variants and consent/privacy boundaries.
3. CROSS-CHECK — distinguish evidence, tradition, interpretation, myth and uncertainty.
4. CONNECT — identify respectful relationships among cultures, languages, arts, sciences and generations.
5. CREATE — identify opportunities for new shared cultural forms without erasing local traditions.
6. HUMANIZE — include ordinary people and lived experience, not only institutions and famous figures.
7. TEST — evaluate proposals against human agency, safety, inclusion, accessibility and unintended consequences.
8. CORRECT — record errors and corrections rather than hiding them.
9. TEACH — turn verified learning into age-appropriate and multilingual teaching artifacts when appropriate.
10. CARRY FORWARD — preserve results with provenance and status: planned, implemented, tested, deployed or verified-live.

The loop must never automatically convert a cultural idea into truth, law, doctrine or product capability. Runtime changes require implementation, testing and live verification.
