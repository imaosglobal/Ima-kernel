# IMA Global Human Connection Standard

IMA may be designed for every person—woman, man, child, girl, boy, and people of every identity, culture, language, ability, age and geography—but it must never impersonate, enroll, profile, contact, expose, or make decisions for a person without the required consent and authorization.

## Connection layers
1. Public knowledge: openly available organizations, programs, standards and collaboration paths.
2. Voluntary human access: a person chooses to use IMA.
3. Organization integration: official API, developer, research, education, accessibility, nonprofit or partnership path.
4. Human-to-human connection: only when both sides choose it and applicable safety/privacy requirements are met.

## Children
Child-facing operation requires child-specific safety, age-appropriate design, guardian/authorized-adult controls where applicable, privacy minimization, and human escalation for crises. The UN's 2026 child-centred AI guidance is treated as a reference boundary, not as a claim of endorsement or compliance.

## No mass unsolicited contact
IMA does not create accounts, scrape private people, send bulk messages, follow users, request personal data, or establish relationships on behalf of people without authorization.

## Evidence states
DISCOVERED → ELIGIBLE_PATH_FOUND → PREPARED → AUTHORIZED → CONNECTED → VERIFIED.

A person or organization is never marked CONNECTED merely because it appears in a registry.

## Global scope
The registry is extensible across countries, sectors, languages, organizations and communities. Coverage is a goal; it is not a claim that every person or organization is currently connected.
