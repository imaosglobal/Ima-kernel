# IMA Research Network Gap Taxonomy

Status: active specification
Parent orchestrator: existing Unified Daily IMA Global Health Loop

Every cycle should ask what is missing before asking what is new.

## Gap classes

- DISCIPLINE_GAP — a scientific or humanistic field is absent or weakly represented.
- EVIDENCE_GAP — a claim lacks primary evidence or independent corroboration.
- COUNTEREVIDENCE_GAP — opposing evidence or interpretation has not been checked.
- GEOGRAPHY_GAP — knowledge is overrepresented by a region or language.
- CULTURE_GAP — local, indigenous, community or cultural knowledge is absent where appropriate.
- ACCESS_GAP — disabled, low-bandwidth, low-resource or otherwise underserved users are not represented.
- DEMOGRAPHIC_GAP — relevant human perspectives are missing without inferring sensitive identity.
- DATA_GAP — an important dataset, measurement stream or API is missing.
- RESEARCHER_GAP — relevant public expertise is not represented.
- METHOD_GAP — alternative methods or replication evidence are missing.
- TIME_GAP — evidence is stale or historical context is missing.
- TRANSLATION_GAP — important knowledge is unavailable across languages.
- IMPLEMENTATION_GAP — a validated idea has no practical pathway.
- ETHICS_GAP — foreseeable ethical, privacy, safety or governance questions remain unanswered.
- PROVENANCE_GAP — source, version, authorship or chain of evidence is unclear.

## Required cycle

DISCOVER -> MAP -> QUESTION -> SEEK COUNTEREVIDENCE -> VERIFY -> CONNECT -> RECORD -> RECHECK.

## Researcher connection boundary

Discovery of a public researcher is not contact.

IMA may:
- identify public professional profiles;
- map publications and expertise;
- identify public institutional contact routes;
- propose possible relevance.

IMA must not:
- infer private attributes;
- expose private contact information;
- claim endorsement or collaboration;
- send unsolicited outreach;
- store credentials.

Direct outreach requires an authorized human action or an explicitly authorized integration with appropriate consent.

## Quality rule

More sources do not automatically mean better knowledge. Prefer independent, primary, reproducible and appropriately current evidence, and preserve disagreement when evidence genuinely conflicts.
