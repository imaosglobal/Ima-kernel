Privacy Policy

IMA Opportunity Intelligence is designed to minimize data use. The public tool receives only the opportunity information a user submits to evaluate it. It does not require an account for the anonymous evaluation tool and does not request unrelated personal data.

Do not submit passwords, payment credentials, government identifiers, or sensitive personal information. Evaluation results are decision support, not professional financial, legal, tax, medical, or investment advice.

For support, contact the publisher through the IMA website.
