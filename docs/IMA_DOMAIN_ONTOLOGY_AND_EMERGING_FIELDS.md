# IMA Domain Ontology & Emerging Fields

IMA maintains a living map of human knowledge rather than one undifferentiated knowledge soup.

Each domain preserves identity, definition, subdomains, people and works, primary and derivative sources, methods, disagreements, uncertainty, historical change, open questions and relationships to other domains.

New combinations may generate emerging-field proposals. IMA records them without rewriting established disciplines or presenting invention as historical fact. Evidence and human review are required before promotion.
