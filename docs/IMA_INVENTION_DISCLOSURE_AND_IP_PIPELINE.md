# IMA Invention Disclosure & Responsible IP Pipeline

**Status:** ACTIVE DESIGN / no patent granted or filed by this document.

## Objective

Turn genuinely new IMA technical mechanisms into evidence-backed invention disclosures for review by qualified patent counsel, while avoiding false novelty claims and preserving IMA's human-centered mission.

## Legal boundary

Patent inventorship is attributed to qualifying natural-person inventors. AI may assist a human inventor but is not itself a substitute for human inventorship. Filing, ownership, assignment and licensing require the appropriate human or legal entity and official evidence.

No patent application is considered filed until an actual official filing receipt or equivalent evidence exists.

## Candidate invention families

### IP-001 — Evidence-bound human-agency control plane for heterogeneous AI

Potential mechanism: a provider-neutral control plane that represents consequential AI capabilities with explicit capability state plus evidence state, binds action authorization to current user/device/context policy, distinguishes simulation from reality, requires human override/recovery paths, and prevents user-facing claims from exceeding verified deployment evidence.

Prior-art warning: agent orchestration, approval-bound execution, provenance and AI governance are already crowded. Initial search found US20260220136A1, US20260244749A1, US12192372B2 and US20260220297A1 as relevant examples. A professional claim chart is required before any filing decision.

### IP-002 — Consent-gated universal interaction memory with private/public learning separation

Potential mechanism: a canonical interaction-memory pipeline requiring explicit per-user consent before capture, preserving integrity verification and deletion/export controls, isolating user memory, and preventing private interaction data from becoming generalized learning without a separate authorization.

Prior-art warning: consent-aware AI memory and multi-agent consent systems already exist, including US20250348618A1 and US20250373575A1. Any IMA claim must identify a concrete technical distinction.

### IP-003 — Human-centered world-model bridge with simulation/reality enforcement

Potential mechanism: a provider-neutral adapter that labels interactive world-model outputs as simulation, preserves provenance, blocks high-impact use without real-world validation, enforces human override/recovery, adapts outputs to device/accessibility constraints, and records the evidence state required before promotion to operational use.

Novelty risk: provenance, authorization, simulation and safety gates each have substantial prior art. The candidate is worth filing investigation only if the combined mechanism demonstrates a non-obvious technical improvement.

### IP-004 — Evidence-state deployment truth protocol

Potential mechanism: a machine-readable capability registry distinguishing implementation, reproducible testing, deployment, live verification, authorization and boundary evidence, with runtime or release controls preventing public claims from exceeding the strongest verified state.

Novelty risk: governance and observability systems are crowded. A filing would require a concrete technical enforcement mechanism rather than a documentation taxonomy.

## Required evidence before filing recommendation

1. Freeze a dated invention disclosure.
2. Record named human contributors and their actual technical contributions.
3. Preserve source commits, tests, architecture and experimental results.
4. Search WIPO, USPTO, EPO and relevant patent databases.
5. Build a claim chart against closest prior art.
6. Identify novelty and non-obviousness risks.
7. Check ownership, employment/contract obligations and open-source licenses.
8. Choose patent, defensive publication, trade secret or open-standard treatment.
9. Obtain qualified patent-counsel review.
10. Only then authorize filing.

## Revenue design

If an IMA patent is ultimately granted and licensed, the financial ledger should track licensee, covered rights/countries, usage metric, royalty formula, gross receipts, verified costs, creator allocation, mission allocation, community/user benefit allocation, reserve/legal allocation and audit evidence.

No royalty income, customer, patent grant, ROI or payment is ever inferred. A real revenue event requires contractual and financial evidence.

## Community value principle

Future licensing revenue may be allocated only according to a ratified governance and financial policy. The intended loop is sustainable technology adoption funding IMA's mission plus a transparent, lawful share of defined benefits for the IMA community, without promising dividends or ownership that do not legally exist.

## Current conclusion

These are invention candidates, not patents. Initial searching shows that the field is already heavily occupied. The strongest path is to patent only concrete mechanisms demonstrated in IMA code and experiments, while using standards or defensive publication for broadly beneficial interoperability where exclusivity would conflict with IMA's mission.
