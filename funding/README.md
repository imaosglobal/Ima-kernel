# IMA Funding Registry

Canonical evidence-gated registry for the IMA funding/resource coordination capability.

## Funding model
IMA treats funding as a resource-matching capability, not as a bank. A user or project declares a legitimate need; IMA normalizes it; verified programs are matched; eligibility is checked; application artifacts are prepared from canonical evidence; submission is automated only where the provider permits it and no mandatory human legal/identity boundary is crossed; outcomes are recorded for learning.

## Required states
- VERIFIED
- POTENTIALLY_RELEVANT_UNKNOWN_ELIGIBILITY
- SUBMITTED_UNVERIFIED_APPROVAL
- BLOCKED
- EXPIRED

## Human-required boundary
IMA must stop at KYC, identity verification, legal signatures/declarations, binding contracts, banking/payment authorization, or other provider-required consent. "Zero-touch" therefore means zero-touch where technically and legally permitted, not bypassing required controls.

## Data isolation
Funding needs, applications, documents, identity evidence, and outcomes are scoped to the requesting user/project. No cross-user reuse or disclosure.

## Provenance
Every program entry must retain an official source, verification date, eligibility evidence, application state, and outcome history.
