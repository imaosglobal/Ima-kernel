# IMA Adaptive Eligibility Core — AE-001

## Purpose
IMA adapts to changing legitimate conditions instead of forcing every person into one fixed route. "Eligibility" means the conditions required for a route, capability, interface, offer or next step to be appropriate and available; it does not mean IMA independently decides legal, medical, tax, benefits or platform eligibility.

## Universal adaptation loop
identify conditions → inspect evidence → identify gaps → adapt route → verify consequential conditions → act only with consent → record outcome → learn → reuse

## Domains
AE-001 applies across accessibility; language and RTL/LTR; identity and consent; capability and runtime; privacy and safety; revenue and payment; benefits and reporting; legal and tax; browser/device; research and organizational pilots.

## Revenue application
Every revenue step includes a route-fit check: payer access, recipient status, terms/reporting, and alternative legitimate routes. Ori's personal Bit route remains governed by the Income & Rights Guard and is never described as tax-deductible, business income, benefit-safe or legally exempt without verification.

## Evidence states
**verified** — authoritative/direct evidence; **user-stated** — supplied but not independently verified; **unknown** — insufficient evidence; **blocked** — route cannot satisfy condition; **adaptable** — another legitimate route may satisfy it. Unknown is never silently converted into eligible.

## Hard boundaries
No invented eligibility; no sensitive-attribute inference; no legal/benefits/tax/medical claims without authoritative verification; no silent consent; no automatic bank/payment/legal-status changes; no rejection when a legitimate alternative can be adapted; human agency remains final for consequential action.

## Canonical integration
AE-001 is part of the single IMA spine and is consumed by the master runtime, public UI, revenue funnel, accessibility/localization, device/browser paths and future integrations.
