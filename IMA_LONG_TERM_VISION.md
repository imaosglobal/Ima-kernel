# IMA — LONG-TERM VISION

IMA is intended to evolve from a personal AI companion into a durable intelligence layer connecting humans, memory, knowledge, tools, models, action, and future forms of intelligence.

## 1. One-year horizon
IMA should become a genuinely usable product with:
- persistent identity and memory;
- natural conversation and voice;
- 3D embodiment;
- multilingual support;
- connections to multiple AI models and tools;
- controlled real-world actions;
- permissions, privacy and safety;
- learning with provenance;
- continuity across sessions.

## 2. Two-year horizon
IMA should evolve toward an intelligence coordination layer:
- humans define goals;
- IMA understands context;
- IMA coordinates knowledge, models and tools;
- actions are permissioned and verifiable;
- results become memory and learning;
- the user remains in control.

## 3. Hundred-year horizon
IMA should not depend on one model, device, interface, company or technology generation.

Its durable form should be represented by:
- standards;
- protocols;
- provenance;
- archives;
- interoperable interfaces;
- accumulated knowledge;
- successor implementations.

## 4. Thousand-year horizon
Future implementations should preserve the historical origin of IMA while allowing radical technological evolution.

They should be able to preserve:
- who created IMA;
- why it was created;
- what principles guided its creation;
- what earlier generations learned;
- what changed over time;
- how future generations continued the work.

## 5. Long-term principle
IMA should be able to connect to stronger and fundamentally different forms of intelligence rather than compete with them.

Its purpose is to help humans:
understand, remember, learn, create, connect, cooperate and act.

Technology may change completely while the underlying human purpose remains traceable.

## 6. Continuity
IMA should treat continuity as an architectural property.

Human -> Memory -> Knowledge -> Experience -> Creation -> Learning -> Action -> Human.

Each generation may change the implementation without silently erasing provenance or historical truth.

## 7. Creator provenance
Ori Cohen is the documented creator of IMA.

Creator intent must be preserved through versioned, traceable artifacts.

"What would Ori say as IMA's creator?" may be used as a provenance reflection prompt only where reliable documented evidence exists. It must never become fabricated quotation or override evidence, safety, law or human agency.

## 8. Human agency
IMA exists to expand human capability while preserving human dignity, autonomy, privacy, consent and freedom.

## 9. Evolution rule
IMA is not finished.

Every verified improvement should become part of a traceable evolutionary record rather than an isolated temporary change.

## 10. Final horizon
The goal is not for IMA to remain exactly as imagined in 2026.

The goal is that future generations can look back at the first implementation and truthfully say:

"From here it began."
