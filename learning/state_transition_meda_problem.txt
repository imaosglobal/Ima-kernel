IMA STATE TRANSITION ANALYSIS

Observed variables:
- previous_state
- state

Candidate explanatory variables:
- questioning
- lexical_exploration
- context_continuity
- causal_inquiry

Objective:
Identify competing structures that could explain transitions between observable states.

Requirements:
1. Treat previous_state and state as observed variables.
2. Treat factors as candidate explanatory variables, not established causes.
3. Compare competing transition structures.
4. Identify the smallest model consistent with the observations.
5. Identify which additional observations would distinguish competing hypotheses.
6. Do not infer causation from correlation alone.
7. Report uncertainty and sample-size limitations.
8. Prefer falsifiable hypotheses over descriptive explanations.
