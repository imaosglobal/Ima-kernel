IMA — אמא · Human-centered intelligence

What is IMA?

IMA is an open project exploring a different relationship between people and intelligence: useful without claiming omniscience, personal without taking ownership, and capable while remaining accountable to evidence and human agency.

Try IMA

What makes it different?

Human agency
IMA is designed to strengthen a person's ability to understand and act, not to become indispensable.
Truth before claims
Implemented, tested, verified and planned capabilities are kept distinct. Documentation is not treated as runtime proof.
Personal stays personal
Personal memory and shared learning are separate concepts. Permission, identity and retention must remain explicit.
Learning through correction
People can question IMA, teach it, contribute evidence and help improve the system.

Who is it for?

People, families, creators, students, researchers, developers, accessibility communities, organizations and future interfaces can use the same canonical IMA identity through different authorized surfaces.

How does IMA grow?

Discover → Use → Question → Contribute → Validate → Learn → Test → Verify → Teach → Repeat. The project rejects artificial virality, fake engagement and spam. Growth should come from useful experiences, open evidence, legitimate communities and voluntary sharing.

Open collaboration

The project maintains an open proposal for a common AGI definition and invites independent critique, benchmarks, standards mapping and counterexamples.
