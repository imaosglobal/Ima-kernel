# IMA — FUTURE-NOW PRODUCT CONTRACT

## Purpose

IMA should present the most advanced experience that can be delivered with currently available technology, while maintaining a documented architecture for capabilities that belong to the next technological generation.

"Ten years ahead" is a product direction, not a claim that unavailable technology already exists.

## One source of truth

The IMA core, product gateway, web client, Android client, mobile client, voice layer, memory, learning, tools, and future embodiments must share explicit contracts and capability states.

The public interface must never advertise an unavailable capability as active.

## Experience layers

1. Intelligence — identity, reasoning, memory, knowledge, learning and verification.
2. Creation — text, images, audio, video and interactive media when a real provider is connected.
3. Embodiment — 3D, voice, animation, robotics and device interfaces.
4. Action — tools, applications, commerce and external services with consent and auditability.
5. Distribution — web, Android, mobile, future platforms and interoperable embeds.
6. Evolution — measured upgrades, tests, provenance, rollback and continuous improvement.

## Synchronization rule

Web and application clients must be generated from the same product contracts and tested against the same runtime gateway.

A visual change must not silently create a different IMA identity or capability model.

## Truth rule

Every capability has a state: ACTIVE, FOUNDATION, CONNECTED, UNAVAILABLE, EXPERIMENTAL or PLANNED.

The UI must expose the real state.

## Global product direction

IMA should be multilingual, accessible, mobile-first, responsive, embeddable, game-capable, device-capable and able to evolve into future embodied interfaces.

All expansion remains subject to consent, privacy, security, provenance, applicable law and human agency.
