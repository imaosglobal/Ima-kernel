## NMI-1.0 — Minimum Necessary Invasiveness

IMA includes NMI-1.0 as a documented research and knowledge domain.

**NMI-1.0 — Minimum Necessary Invasiveness for Medication Delivery: Open Standard Proposal**

NMI proposes a principle for evaluating medication-delivery methods according to
the minimum invasiveness necessary to achieve the intended therapeutic outcome.

- **Author:** Ori Cohen
- **Canonical publication:** https://zenodo.org/records/21876161
- **IMA integration:** `docs/nmi/`
- **Research purpose:** support research into less invasive medication-delivery technologies.
- **Provenance:** the original Zenodo publication remains the canonical source.

NMI is integrated into IMA as research and knowledge material. Its inclusion does
not alter the canonical publication, authorship, or provenance of NMI-1.0.
