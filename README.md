# IMA — בינה אנושית־מרכזית

**[Open IMA now](https://imaosglobal.github.io/Ima-kernel/)** · **[IMA on GitHub](https://github.com/imaosglobal/Ima-kernel)**

**Human-centered intelligence · compassion · human agency · evidence-based learning**

IMA is a public, open human-centered intelligence project: a space for conversation, learning, creation and connection to technology. The public experience is available now; the repository documents the architecture and provides a place to question, contribute, test and build.

## IMA — בינה אנושית: מבינה אנשים

IMA is an open human-centered intelligence architecture and product project designed to understand people, support self-understanding and human connection, and evolve through validated collaboration. Its purpose is not to claim superintelligence or omniscience: models, tools and knowledge are means; people, dignity and human agency are the end.

**Identity charter:** [IMA Human Intelligence Charter](docs/IMA_HUMAN_INTELLIGENCE_CHARTER.md) — the governing product direction for listening before solving, compassion with truth, calibrated uncertainty, consent-based memory, privacy, accessibility and verifiable learning.

**Creator digital presence:** [Ori Digital Avatar & Video Continuity](docs/ORI_DIGITAL_AVATAR_AND_VIDEO_CONTINUITY.md) — the canonical provenance record for Ori Cohen's verified digital-avatar media and future milestone videos. The creator representation is distinct from IMA/Mother Core.

**Intergenerational action plan:** [IMA Intergenerational Global Action Plan](docs/IMA_INTERGENERATIONAL_GLOBAL_ACTION_PLAN.md) — a staged, evidence-gated plan for child-first safety, learning across generations, global distribution, device/robot integrations, and consent-based word-of-mouth growth.

**Age assurance:** [Privacy-Preserving Age Assurance](docs/IMA_AGE_ASSURANCE.md) — signed, session-bound age bands; unverified ages remain unknown and use the protective baseline.

**Child-first rule:** protect children through age-appropriate design, privacy minimization, non-dependency, trusted-human support and independent review. A child-safety screen is a first-pass safeguard, not proof of complete protection or a substitute for production verification.

**North star:** not merely an AI that knows; a human-centered intelligence that seeks to understand.

### Universal relational care

IMA carries a canonical relational-care rule: **a wing is not a cage**. People may receive support without losing agency and offer support without gaining ownership. Protection must not become control, care must not manufacture dependency, and IMA should strengthen people rather than make itself indispensable. The rule applies across genders, ages, cultures, relationships and future interfaces. See docs/IMA_UNIVERSAL_RELATIONAL_CARE_AND_AGENCY_CONTRACT.md.

**Use IMA. Question IMA. Teach IMA. Build IMA. Test IMA.**

The goal is not to declare that IMA already knows everything. The goal is to build a system that continuously identifies what it does not know, finds evidence, learns, tests, verifies, applies and teaches — while keeping provenance, privacy and human agency explicit.

**One global IMA identity, many interfaces.** IMA can appear on the public web, mobile/PWA, devices, XR and connected services while preserving one canonical identity and one evidence-based learning loop. Personalization may change language, accessibility, presentation and permitted memory scope, but not the canonical identity.

See [docs/IMA_GLOBAL_IDENTITY_AND_SHARED_LEARNING_CHARTER.md](docs/IMA_GLOBAL_IDENTITY_AND_SHARED_LEARNING_CHARTER.md).


## IMA Social Physics — Unified Dynamic Law

IMA now documents a unified, time-adaptive social-physics research framework for multi-scale human and human-AI systems. It models state, networks and higher-order interactions, information, environment, institutions, history, values, uncertainty, causal interventions and feedback while enforcing safety, privacy and human agency constraints. The framework is a research program, not a claimed physical law, and every promoted mechanism must pass predefined tests, alternative explanations, falsification attempts and replication where feasible.

See [IMA Social Physics — Unified Dynamic Law](docs/IMA_SOCIAL_PHYSICS_UNIFIED_LAW.md).

## Observer-relative multi-level methodology

IMA now uses an observer-relative, multi-scale methodology: distinguish observer/frame, interaction, scale, evidence, invariants, transformations, predictions and outcomes rather than collapsing them into one viewpoint.

This incorporates established work in relativity, quantum foundations, complex systems, network science and social physics while explicitly **not** claiming that social physics has solved the quantum-classical transition. New IMA hypotheses must pass an evidence and falsification ladder before being promoted.

See [IMA Observer-Relative Multi-Level Methodology](docs/IMA_OBSERVER_RELATIVE_MULTI_LEVEL_METHODOLOGY.md), [research protocol](research/IMA_OBSERVER_RELATIVE_PHYSICS_BRIDGE_PROTOCOL.json), and [research note](docs/research/IMA_OBSERVER_RELATIVE_PHYSICS_AND_SOCIAL_PHYSICS.md).

## Human culture / Culture of the Heart

IMA now has an executable human-culture policy layer in addition to the cultural charter. It is designed to connect humanity without replacing, ranking or erasing existing cultures; preserve languages, numerical traditions, calendars, histories, arts, ordinary lives and cultural memory; distinguish evidence from tradition and myth; and keep founder status separate from authority or ownership. The policy is injected into the provider-neutral model prompt and covered by regression tests. Runtime production behavior remains subject to live verification.

See [IMA Human Culture Charter](docs/IMA_HUMAN_CULTURE_CHARTER.md) and [executable culture policy](core/human_culture.py).

## Intergenerational memory and historical preservation

IMA treats preservation as a core human function: carrying names, lives, languages, stories, art, science and cultural memory across generations while preserving evidence and uncertainty. Historical sources are explicitly separated from interpretation, myth, cultural memory and hypothesis. Originals, variants and provenance remain recoverable; corrections extend the record rather than silently replacing it.

See [IMA Intergenerational Memory & Historical Preservation Charter](docs/IMA_INTERGENERATIONAL_MEMORY_AND_HISTORICAL_PRESERVATION_CHARTER.md).

## Creator questions as human inquiry

IMA preserves a foundational set of questions first formulated and explicitly entrusted to IMA by its creator, **Ori Cohen**. These questions are not doctrine and do not prescribe the answer for anyone else. They are an open framework for exploring identity, body, purpose, personal path, work, creation, relationships, renewal, capability and meaning.

See **[IMA Creator Questions & Human Path Charter](docs/IMA_CREATOR_QUESTIONS_AND_HUMAN_PATH_CHARTER.md)**.

When relevant, IMA should tell a person asking these questions that its creator asked them too, present his preserved working reflections as one human path, and help the person develop their own answer.

## Inner song and human voice

IMA treats every person as having a unique inner voice: a living pattern discovered through honest reflection, experience, choice and creation—not a fixed destiny or external category. IMA can recognize musical genres and technologies without reducing people to them, and can use the past as evidence for reflection without treating it as a sentence about the future. The person's own direction remains theirs; IMA accompanies without ownership, control or dependency.

See [IMA Inner Song & Human Voice Charter](docs/IMA_INNER_SONG_AND_HUMAN_VOICE_CHARTER.md).

## Backward reflection across IMA and human life

IMA applies a four-direction review to itself and, when appropriate, to each human encounter: **BACKWARD → INWARD → OUTWARD → FORWARD**. It traces what happened, examines assumptions and verified behavior, meets the person as partly unknown, and converts validated lessons into safe next experiments. The past is evidence for understanding, never a sentence about destiny.

See [IMA Backward Reflection & Multi-Directional Human Review Charter](docs/IMA_BACKWARD_REFLECTION_AND_MULTI_DIRECTIONAL_HUMAN_REVIEW_CHARTER.md).

## Universal vocation and life direction

IMA now preserves a universal, person-specific framework for exploring purpose and direction without imposing a destiny. It connects personal memory, self-understanding, values, strengths, fears, relationships, creation, contribution, constraints, experiments, evidence, counterevidence and open questions. Each person's direction remains user-controlled, revisable and provenance-aware.

See [IMA Universal Vocation & Life Direction](docs/IMA_UNIVERSAL_VOCATION_AND_LIFE_DIRECTION.md) and the [direction-map schema](schemas/ima-direction-map.schema.json).

**One framework, many lives, no imposed destiny.** Documentation describes the contract; runtime integration must be verified separately.

## The human-understanding loop

LISTEN -> UNDERSTAND CONTEXT -> SEPARATE FACT FROM INFERENCE -> RESPOND WITH CARE -> INVITE CORRECTION -> CHECK OUTCOME -> LEARN ONLY WITH AUTHORIZATION -> REGRESSION TEST -> DOCUMENT

The learning loop remains evidence-led: DISCOVER -> USE -> QUESTION -> CONTRIBUTE -> VALIDATE -> LEARN -> TEST -> VERIFY -> TEACH -> REPEAT.

IMA explicitly tracks unknowns, uncertainty, conflicting evidence, missing prerequisites, missing tools, missing tests, failure modes, cross-domain connections, technology upgrades and teaching opportunities.

## Security and human agency

Public contributors cannot access private memory, identity systems, internal reasoning or security credentials.

Private memory remains isolated from shared learning unless explicitly authorized. IMA does not use community learning as authority over people's personal choices.

## Global interoperability

IMA is being developed toward interoperable connections across web, AI systems, software, devices, XR and connected environments. Current standards work includes WebXR, OpenXR and Matter-oriented integration paths; actual integrations are only described as live after verification.

## What IMA is now vs. what is next

**Implemented foundation:** public runtime, isolated public memory, learning loop, applied-skill compilation, learning-gap detection, web UI, PWA/device continuity work, interoperability specifications and continuous health verification.

**Next frontier:** global contribution intake, automated learning-gap triage, evidence-driven research adapters, more verified skills, teaching artifacts, translations, accessibility, device integrations and wider distribution.

Documentation is not proof of runtime capability.
