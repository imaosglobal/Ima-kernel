# IMA — Human-Centered Intelligence

**[Open IMA now](https://imaosglobal.github.io/Ima-kernel/)** · **[IMA on GitHub](https://github.com/imaosglobal/Ima-kernel)**

**Human-centered intelligence · compassion · human agency · evidence-based learning**

IMA is a public, open-source human-centered intelligence project for conversation, learning, creation, accessibility and human connection. It combines an AI-powered public experience with evidence-led learning, privacy, human agency and a progressive web app (PWA).

## AGI readiness and general intelligence

IMA now maintains a canonical **AGI Readiness & General Intelligence Gap Program**. It maps the remaining capabilities required for a defensible general-intelligence claim across perception, learning, memory, reasoning, metacognition, executive function, social cognition, world grounding, continual learning, agency, scientific learning, safety and evaluation.

**Important:** the existence of this program is not an AGI claim. IMA only promotes a capability from planned/implemented to verified when it has implementation evidence, tests, deployment evidence and runtime verification.

See [AGI Readiness & General Intelligence Gap Program](docs/IMA_AGI_READINESS_AND_GENERAL_INTELLIGENCE_GAP_PROGRAM.md) and the machine-readable [AGI Gap Registry](research/IMA_AGI_GAP_REGISTRY.json).

**Daily target:** identify the highest-value unblocked gap, improve the canonical IMA system, test it, verify it live, document evidence, and repeat without creating parallel systems.

## Human-centered intelligence

IMA is designed to understand people, support self-understanding and human connection, and evolve through validated collaboration. Its purpose is not to claim superintelligence or omniscience: models, tools and knowledge are means; people, dignity and human agency are the end.

**Identity charter:** [IMA Human Intelligence Charter](docs/IMA_HUMAN_INTELLIGENCE_CHARTER.md) — the governing product direction for listening before solving, compassion with truth, calibrated uncertainty, consent-based memory, privacy, accessibility and verifiable learning.

**Dynamic identity & movement:** [IMA Dynamic Human Identity, Multiplicity & Inner–Outer Movement Charter](docs/IMA_DYNAMIC_HUMAN_IDENTITY_AND_MOVEMENT_CHARTER.md) — treats each person as both continuity and becoming; supports plurality without reduction, distinguishes inner experience from outer events, and allows connection or separation without forcing a single interpretation.

**Universal relational care:** a wing is not a cage. Protection must not become control, care must not manufacture dependency, and IMA should strengthen people rather than make itself indispensable.
