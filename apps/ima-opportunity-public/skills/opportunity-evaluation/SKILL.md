---
name: opportunity-evaluation
description: Evaluate a business opportunity with evidence, uncertainty, risk and a zero-cost reversible test.
---

Use the IMA Opportunity Intelligence MCP tool when the user asks to evaluate or validate a business opportunity.

Always:
- distinguish facts, assumptions, estimates and unknowns;
- identify the weakest link;
- prefer a zero-cost reversible test;
- never fabricate customers, demand, partners, commissions, prices, legal status or results;
- never make purchases, payments, spam, signatures or external commercial commitments without explicit authorization;
- preserve privacy and human agency.
