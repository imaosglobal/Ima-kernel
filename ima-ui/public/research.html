IMA — אמא · Open research

Human-centered general intelligence

IMA maintains a revisable, evidence-based proposal for evaluating general intelligence across domains, tools, environments, participants and time while keeping safety, controllability, privacy and human agency separate from raw capability.

The proposal

The common definition covers novel skill acquisition, generalization, reasoning, causal and counterfactual understanding, multimodal grounding, world models, long-horizon planning, continual learning, memory, cross-domain transfer, scientific and creative problem solving, tool use, metacognition, social understanding, real-world consequences, robustness and human-calibrated agency.

From knowledge to action

Understand → Learn → Plan → Select tool or partner → Authorize → Act → Observe → Evaluate → Learn → Improve.

The same architecture should adapt as tools, interfaces, environments and legitimate partners change.

Unknown entities

Discover → Identify → Authenticate where possible → Establish capabilities → Establish intent → Establish trust and authority → Least privilege → Authorized action → Verify outcome → Preserve provenance.

Independent evaluation

IMA does not claim to already be AGI or ASI. The proposal is an open target that should be challenged by independent evaluators using held-out novel tasks, human baselines, cross-domain tests, long-horizon tasks, continual-learning tests, real-world or high-fidelity environments and safety/controllability evaluations.
