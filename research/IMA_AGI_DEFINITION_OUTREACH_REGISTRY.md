## v1.1 focus

The update explicitly adds contextual and personalized intelligence, identity/intent/capability/authority separation, lifelong continuity, unknown-agent interaction, knowledge-to-action transfer, changing tool/environment adaptation, and interoperable human-machine cooperation.

## Rules

- Never claim consensus from outreach alone.
- Prefer public, relevant, organization-controlled contact channels.
- Record replies, objections, proposed amendments, and evidence.
- Add organizations only when relevance is established.
- Do not mass-mail or spam.
- When a materially relevant definition or standard changes, compare it against IMA-AGI-COMMON and update versioned artifacts together.
- If a stronger shared definition emerges, update the definition, evaluation, architecture, roadmap and outreach registry as one change set.
- Preserve disagreement and uncertainty rather than manufacturing consensus.
