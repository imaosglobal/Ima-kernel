## Rules

- Never claim consensus from outreach alone.
- Prefer public, relevant, organization-controlled contact channels.
- Record replies, objections, proposed amendments, and evidence.
- Add organizations only when relevance is established.
- Do not mass-mail or spam.
- If a stronger shared definition emerges, compare it against IMA-AGI-COMMON and update versioned artifacts together.
