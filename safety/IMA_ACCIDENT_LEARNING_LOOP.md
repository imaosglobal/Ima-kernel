# IMA Accident Prevention & Learning Loop

## Core rule

Every incident, near miss, detected hazard and recovery should become an opportunity for safer future behavior, without turning unverified assumptions into rules.

### Pipeline

1. Detect
2. Protect / stabilize
3. Alert appropriate people
4. Preserve evidence and provenance
5. Separate facts from hypotheses
6. Analyze contributing factors
7. Generate prevention options
8. Validate in simulation, review or controlled testing
9. Deploy within authority boundaries
10. Monitor outcomes
11. Audit
12. Feed validated lessons into the knowledge base

### Safety controls

- least privilege
- independent device-side safety
- human override
- emergency stop where applicable
- rate and force limits
- geofencing where applicable
- conflict detection
- uncertainty thresholds
- degraded/offline modes
- rollback
- audit logs
- privacy-preserving incident records

### Recovery mode

If prevention fails, switch from prevention to resilience:
stabilize → communicate → coordinate → document → assist recovery → learn → prevent recurrence.

This applies to individuals, families, communities, organizations, countries and cross-border/global systems, with appropriate legal and professional boundaries.
