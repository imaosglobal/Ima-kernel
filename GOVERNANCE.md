# IMA Governance

IMA is an open project by Ori Cohen intended to grow through contributions from people and systems worldwide.

## Core principles

1. Human agency remains central.
2. Original works and canonical artifacts are not silently overwritten.
3. Derivatives preserve provenance and attribution.
4. Changes should be additive, reversible and verifiable where practical.
5. Secrets, private information and credentials must never enter the repository.
6. Safety, verification and provenance mechanisms must not be disabled merely to make a contribution pass.
7. Contributors retain attribution for their work.
8. Technical disagreement is resolved through evidence, reproducible tests and documented reasoning.
9. The canonical vision remains identifiable even as implementations evolve.
10. Long-term preservation and portability are first-class project concerns.

## Contribution flow

Issue → discussion/implementation → tests → pull request → review → merge.

Small independent contributions are encouraged.

## Maintainer responsibility

Maintainers should preserve project continuity, review contributions fairly, protect canonical and preservation-critical material, and keep the project understandable to new contributors.

## Evolution

Governance may evolve as the contributor community grows. Changes should themselves be documented and attributable.
