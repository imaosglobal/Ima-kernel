# IMA — Product Hunt launch draft

## Name
IMA — אמא

## Tagline
Human-centered intelligence you can question, teach and test.

## Description
IMA is an open human-centered intelligence layer built around truth, agency and verified learning. Ask a real question, get a useful answer, correct it, teach it, and help shape what comes next. One canonical identity across web, AI and authorized tools.

## Maker story
IMA was built by Ori Cohen from a simple question: what if intelligence helped people become more capable instead of becoming dependent on it?

The project is intentionally open. IMA does not ask people to believe that it already knows everything. It exposes uncertainty, separates personal memory from shared learning, and promotes capabilities only after verification.

## What we want to learn
What would make IMA genuinely useful in your daily life?

## Launch rule
Ask for feedback, not votes. Do not buy engagement or make unverified capability claims.
